Privacy

Privacy notice

Updated 25 October 2026. This independent finder is operated by Tom Völker, who is responsible for its data processing. Contact: emnlp-finder@tomvoelker.de.

Hosting and technical requests

GitHub Pages hosts this site. Requests disclose technical information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information and access time. GitHub logs IP addresses for security. Opening a shared search URL also sends that URL to the host. This processing serves the legitimate interest of providing and protecting the finder under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub manages its security-log retention as described in its privacy statement.

Usage statistics

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits, referrers, countries, browser and device types, and page performance. It uses no analytics cookies or browser storage and does not fingerprint individual visitors. Query strings are excluded from its reports. Cloudflare keeps unsampled beacon data for seven days, then samples it for longer-term storage. Reports cover the previous six months. See its analytics FAQ.

A separate Cloudflare Worker counts searches, filter changes, author and paper links, shortlist actions, calendar exports, and external paper and contact links. It stores only an action category, count and timestamp for three months. These counts contain no search text, names, paper IDs, shortlist contents, email addresses or visitor identifiers. Worker request logging is disabled. Cloudflare still handles technical connection data to deliver and protect its services.

The purpose is to understand usage and improve the finder. Where technical personal data is processed for this purpose, the basis is the legitimate interest in operating and improving the site under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Both analytics streams stay off when your browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Browser storage and public programme data

Your shortlist and explicit theme choice stay in this browser’s local storage until you remove them or clear site data. They are not uploaded. If you choose Share list, the link contains the saved paper IDs in its fragment. Anyone with the link can open that snapshot. Opening it does not change their saved papers. Save on this device adds them locally. Search runs in your browser and its current state appears in the URL. Public paper, author and presenter information comes from the conference schedule, ACL Anthology and Underline. It remains available while this finder is online to help people find conference work, a legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Contact me for corrections or data-rights requests.

Email, external links and providers

Email contact is optional. Sending a message shares your address and message with the email services involved. I use it to answer your enquiry under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and retain it only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any applicable legal obligations. The LinkedIn icon is included in this page. LinkedIn receives no request from it until you follow the link.

GitHub and Cloudflare may process technical data outside the EU/EEA, including in the US. Their policies describe transfer safeguards, including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses: GitHub and Cloudflare.
